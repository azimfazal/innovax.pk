FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we start with only a few modules?
Yes. Innovax HMIS is modular. Many hospitals begin with patient registration, OPD and billing, then add laboratory, inpatient and inventory modules later without re-implementing anything.
Is our data kept separate and secure?
Access is controlled by role, and every deployment is installed in your own environment with your own backup strategy. We do not publish or share hospital data, and the marketing site never collects patient information.
Does the laboratory module connect to analyzers?
The LIS supports an analyzer-integration pathway for result capture. Specific analyzer models are confirmed during the discovery phase so we can scope the integration accurately.
How long does implementation take?
It depends on the module set and hospital size. A focused first phase (registration, OPD, billing) is typically live within weeks; full multi-department rollouts are phased over a longer schedule agreed with your team.
Do you provide training and after-go-live support?
Yes. Every rollout includes role-based training and a supervised go-live, followed by ongoing support and continuous improvement of the configured workflows.
